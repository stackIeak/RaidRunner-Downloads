A new army editor, and Raid Runner's own artwork.

- **Army editor:** Clash of Clans pictures for every hero, troop, Super Troop, siege machine and spell. Click a troop or spell to add one, click again or hold to add more, right-click to remove one, and drag them in your army to change their order. The ✕ removes a slot.
- **Artwork:** pictures on the sidebar (Attack, Army, Loot, Upgrades) and next to gold, elixir and dark elixir in Farming Statistics.
- **Status bar:** a new bar while Raid Runner runs - running time, battles, total loot and upgrades.
- **Prefer the old Army page?** Settings > Army page > Classic. Both pages use the same saved armies.

Farming, wall upgrades and every purchase safeguard are unchanged. Your settings, statistics and armies are kept.

**Updating:** Raid Runner V1.2.3 updates itself (Settings > Updates > Check for Updates). New users: download `Raid Runner V1.3.0.zip`, unzip and run the EXE.
