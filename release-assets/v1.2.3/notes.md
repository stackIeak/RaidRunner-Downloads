- **Army presets:** drag an army and it follows your pointer, the others slide out of the way, and it settles smoothly into place. The order is saved at once.

Farming, wall upgrades (including the V1.2.2 discount-price fix) and every purchase safeguard are unchanged. Your settings, statistics and armies are kept.
